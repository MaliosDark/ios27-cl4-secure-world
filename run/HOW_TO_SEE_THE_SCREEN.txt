HOW TO SEE THE IPHONE SCREEN
============================
The VM runs on your Mac. To see the panel:

1) NATIVE WINDOW (simplest):
     cd /Users/maliosdark/darwin-vm && ./view_screen.sh
   -> a window opens with the live 640x1136 panel.

2) OVER VNC (to watch live / remote runs):
     cd /Users/maliosdark/darwin-vm && ./live_view_vnc.sh
   -> serves at vnc://localhost:5900
   -> on macOS: Finder -> Cmd+K -> vnc://localhost:5900  (Screen Sharing)

3) FULL OS (needs your rootfs in darwin-vm/rootfs/):
     ./run_rootfs.sh

Note: the assistant runs headless (no monitor) and captures the screen with
screendump to send it to you as an image. The real window opens on YOUR Mac.
